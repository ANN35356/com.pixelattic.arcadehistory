Privacy Policy

Video Games History (com.pixelattic.arcadehistory) · Last updated: 20 August 2026

MOHAMMED HASSAN (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates Video Games History (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Summary. The App is played fully offline. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any account login.

No internet, no servers

The App works without an internet connection. It does not send data to any server and does not communicate with us over the network.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your game progress and settings (for example: current level, best score, streaks, selected theme, and whether you have seen the tutorial) only on your device, using the device’s local storage. This information:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is not transmitted to us or to any third party,
  • is removed when you uninstall the App.

Permissions

The App does not request access to sensitive device features such as your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or files.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The App does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software. We do not use cookies or device identifiers to track you.

Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we also collect nothing from children. The App complies with children’s privacy standards (including COPPA) by design.

In-app purchases (if offered)

If the App offers an optional purchase, the transaction is processed entirely by the Amazon Appstore. We never receive or store your payment card details. Please see Amazon’s own privacy policy for how Amazon handles payments.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
